Payment Gateway Processor (Generics)
Story: Indian e-commerce platform builds payment gateway with multiple payment types.

Actors:
- Transaction<T>: processPayment(), getTransactionDetails()
- Payment Types: UPI, Card, NetBanking
- Payment Gateway

Story Example:
Ramesh pays via UPI ₹1200 → Successful
Priya pays via Card ₹5000 → Successful
Company pays via NetBanking ₹8000 → Successful

Concepts: Generics, Encapsulation, Polymorphism, Extensibility.
