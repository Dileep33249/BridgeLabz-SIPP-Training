Hospital Department Mapping
Story: Apollo Hospital needs mapping of departments to doctors.

Actors:
- Department (String)
- Doctor (name, specialization, experienceYears)
- Directory: Map<String, List<Doctor>>

Story Example:
Cardiology → [Dr. Mehta (15 yrs), Dr. Kapoor (10 yrs)]
Neurology → [Dr. Sharma (10 yrs), Dr. Das (8 yrs)]
Pediatrics → [Dr. Ritu (12 yrs), Dr. Anjali (7 yrs)]

Query: Neurology → Dr. Sharma, Dr. Das

Concepts: Collections (Map + List), Generics, Encapsulation, Streams.
