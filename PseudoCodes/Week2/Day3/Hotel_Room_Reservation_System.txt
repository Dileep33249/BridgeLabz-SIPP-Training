Hotel Room Reservation System
Story: Sunrise Grand Hotel offers Deluxe Room (₹3000/night) and Suite Room (₹5000/night).

Actors:
- Room (Base Class): roomNumber, customerName, daysStayed, calculateBill()
- DeluxeRoom (Subclass)
- SuiteRoom (Subclass)
- Customer: choose room type and days, receive bill

Story Example:
Ravi books Deluxe Room for 3 nights → Total ₹9000
Anita books Suite Room for 2 nights → Total ₹10000

Concepts: Inheritance, Polymorphism, Encapsulation, Reusability.
